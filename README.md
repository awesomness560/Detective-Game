## About the Game
`A Game Where You Search for Objects While Bantering with a Very Handsome Little Fella` is a short, narrative-driven psychological horror game where you must solve a mystery involving you. You awaken with amnesia in what you're told is a homicide victim's house. Guided by a man named Russo, who claims to be your detective partner, you must search for clues to piece together what happened.

As you uncover each piece of the puzzle, the house subtly transforms, growing more surreal and hostile. The truth is hidden in the colors of your fractured mind, and revealing it will expose the dark reality of who you are, who Russo is, and what really happened in this house.

## Story
You open your eyes on a couch, your mind a complete blank. A stranger, Joshua Russo, tells you that you're his boss, a brilliant detective, and your memory has been temporarily fried. He explains that you're both at the scene of a homicide you were investigating a week ago, looking for a final piece of evidence the victim, a kleptomaniac, had hidden away.

Trusting him, you begin to investigate. But with every clue you find (an occult tome, a burned photograph, a strange receipt) you feel a sense of familiarity. The name you remember isn't your own, and the house seems to be hiding secrets that are inextricably tied to you.

## Gameplay
The game plays as a side-scroller, blending exploration, narrative, and puzzle-solving into a tight loop.

1.  **Explore:** Navigate the 2D environment set within a 3D room to find hidden items.
2.  **Find a Clue:** Discovering an object triggers a memory and a dialogue exchange with Russo.
3.  **Unlock a Color:** Each clue unlocks a new color on your **Mind Map**, a puzzle representing your fragmented psyche.
4.  **Solve the Puzzle:** Complete the `Flow`-like puzzle in the Mind Map to connect your thoughts and make a breakthrough.
5.  **The House Changes:** With each puzzle solved, the environment warps. The lighting flickers, paintings weep, and ghostly apparitions appear, allowing you to access the next clue in the sequence.

This cycle repeats until the final reveal.

## Features
* **A Chilling Mystery:** Uncover the truth of your own murder from a first-person, perspective.
* **Dynamic Environment:** Watch the house change and grow more unsettling as you get closer to the truth.
* **Unique Art Style:** We used 2D assets for characters and environments within a 3D space, creating a distinct look.
* **Atmospheric Storytelling:** A narrative that unfolds through dialogue, environmental clues, and a creepy soundtrack.
* **Mind Map Puzzles:** Engage with color-based puzzles that represent the process of piecing your shattered memories back together.
